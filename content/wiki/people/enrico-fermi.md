> "Where is everybody?"
> <cite>— Enrico Fermi, on the apparent absence of extraterrestrial civilisations</cite>

---

Italian-American physicist and Nobel laureate (1938) who built the world's first nuclear reactor and made seminal contributions to quantum theory, statistical mechanics, and nuclear physics. His casual lunchtime question — "Where is everybody?" — became known as the [[Fermi paradox]], one of the most enduring puzzles in astrobiology and cosmology. The paradox asks why, given the vast number of potentially habitable planets, we have observed no signs of alien civilisations.
