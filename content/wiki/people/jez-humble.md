British software engineer, author, and researcher best known for co-authoring **Continuous Delivery** (2010, with David Farley) — the book that defined and formalized the discipline of releasing software reliably and rapidly. The book distilled lessons from years of working as a technical principal at **ThoughtWorks** on large-scale enterprise delivery projects.

Humble later co-authored **[[../books/accelerate|Accelerate]]** (2018, with [[gene-kim|Gene Kim]] and Nicole Forsgren) — the first rigorous, statistically grounded study of software delivery performance. Accelerate introduced the **DORA metrics** (Deployment Frequency, Lead Time for Changes, Change Failure Rate, Time to Restore) which are now the standard measure of engineering throughput and stability.

## Key contributions

- **Continuous Delivery** (2010, with David Farley): defined the CD pipeline: commit → build → test → acceptance → production; introduced deployment pipelines as a core concept
- **[[../books/accelerate|Accelerate]]** (2018): rigorous research demonstrating that high software delivery performance positively correlates with organizational outcomes and profitability
- **DORA metrics**: the four key metrics now used by engineering leaders worldwide to measure delivery performance
- **Lean Enterprise** (2014, with Joanne Molesky & Barry O'Reilly): applying lean and agile principles to large organizations

## Interesting facts

- Humble got his start hacking on ZX Spectrum and Acorn computers as a child — a background that gave him a deep appreciation for tight feedback loops and rapid iteration
- The *Continuous Delivery* book predated Docker and Kubernetes by several years; its principles proved just as relevant when containers became mainstream
- DORA (DevOps Research and Assessment) was acquired by Google in 2018, and the State of DevOps Report continues to be published under Google Cloud's banner
