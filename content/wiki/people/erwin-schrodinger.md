> "The task is not to see what has never been seen before, but to think what has never been thought before about what you see every day."
> <cite>— Erwin Schrödinger</cite>

---

Austrian physicist who formulated the Schrödinger equation, the central equation of quantum mechanics. His 1935 thought experiment — [[Schrödinger's cat]] — was intended to show the absurdity of the Copenhagen interpretation's superposition principle when applied to macroscopic objects. He shared the 1933 Nobel Prize in Physics with Paul Dirac for the discovery of new productive forms of atomic theory.
