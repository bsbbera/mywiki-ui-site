> # Bill Inmon
> ###### Data Warehousing Pioneer
> | | |
> | --- | --- |
> | **Born** | 1945 |
> | **Nationality** | American |
> | **Domain** | Data warehousing |
> | **Known for** | "Father of the Data Warehouse", top-down EDW |
> | **Notable works** | [[../books/building-the-data-warehouse\|Building the Data Warehouse]] |

American computer scientist often called the **"Father of the Data Warehouse"**. Coined the term and articulated the **top-down enterprise data warehouse (EDW)** approach — third-normal-form integrated repository feeding **data marts**. Counterpart to [[ralph-kimball|Ralph Kimball]]'s bottom-up methodology. Authored *Building the Data Warehouse*. Later proposed the **Data Vault** + **DW 2.0** ideas.
