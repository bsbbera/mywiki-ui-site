> "The essential condition for the existence of a market economy is the existence of a stable currency."
> <cite>— Maurice Allais</cite>

---

French physicist-turned-economist who won the 1988 Nobel Memorial Prize in Economic Sciences for his pioneering contributions to the theory of markets and efficient utilisation of resources. The [[Allais paradox]] — his 1953 demonstration that people's choices violate the independence axiom of expected utility theory — became a cornerstone of behavioural economics and directly influenced the development of prospect theory by Kahneman and Tversky.
