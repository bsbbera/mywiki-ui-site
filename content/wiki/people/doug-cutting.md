> # Doug Cutting
> ###### Software Engineer & Open Source Pioneer
> | | |
> | --- | --- |
> | **Born** | 1963 |
> | **Nationality** | American |
> | **Domain** | Search, big data, open source |
> | **Known for** | Creator of Apache Lucene, Hadoop, Nutch, Avro |
> | **Institution** | ex-Xerox PARC; ex-Apple; ex-Excite@Home; ex-Yahoo!; ex-Cloudera |
> | **Education** | BS, Stanford University |

American software engineer and open-source pioneer. Earned a **BS from Stanford University** and began his career in search technology at **Xerox PARC**, then moved to **Apple** and **Excite@Home**, building early commercial search engines throughout the 1990s. Launched **Apache Lucene** in **2000** as an open-source full-text search library — now the engine underlying Elasticsearch, Apache Solr, and countless search systems worldwide.

Co-created **Apache Nutch** (2002, with Mike Cafarella) — an open-source web crawler and search engine. After reading [[jeff-dean-sanjay-ghemawat|Google's GFS and MapReduce papers]], Cutting implemented open-source equivalents: **HDFS** (Hadoop Distributed File System) and **Hadoop MapReduce**, packaged together as **Apache Hadoop** (2005, named after his son's stuffed toy yellow elephant). Joined **Yahoo!** to lead Hadoop full-time, where it was scaled to thousands of nodes. Joined **Cloudera** (2009) when the company was founded to commercialize Hadoop enterprise support. Also created **Apache Avro** — a compact, fast binary data serialization framework.

## Key contributions

- **Apache Lucene** (2000): dominant open-source text search library
- **Apache Nutch** (2002, with Mike Cafarella): open-source web crawler
- **Apache Hadoop** (2005): open-source HDFS + MapReduce — the foundation of the big-data era
- **Apache Avro**: compact binary data serialization and RPC framework
- Turned Google's proprietary GFS + MapReduce into open-source tools accessible to every company

## Interesting facts

- Named **Hadoop** after his son's stuffed toy yellow elephant — one of the most famous naming stories in open source
- **Lucene** is the underlying engine behind Elasticsearch, Apache Solr, Nutch, and dozens of other search systems — quietly powering search at millions of websites
- Hadoop became the backbone of enterprise data lakes at Facebook, LinkedIn, Twitter, and Yahoo throughout 2008–2015, effectively creating the "big data" industry category
