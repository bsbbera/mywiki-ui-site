> "We must know. We will know."
> <cite>— David Hilbert</cite>

---

German mathematician and one of the most influential figures in mathematics and physics at the turn of the 20th century. His 1900 list of 23 unsolved problems shaped research agendas for decades. In set theory, [[Hilbert's hotel]] is a vivid thought experiment illustrating the counter-intuitive properties of infinite sets. He also made foundational contributions to functional analysis (Hilbert spaces) and the formalist programme in philosophy of mathematics.
