# Isaac Luria

Influential Jewish mystic and rabbi (1534–1572) known as the Ari, who revolutionised Kabbalah with the doctrine of *tzimtzum* — a primordial divine contraction that made room for finite creation. His Lurianic Kabbalah introduced the concepts of shevirat ha-kelim (breaking of the vessels) and tikkun (repair), profoundly shaping Jewish mystical thought and the paradoxes of divine infinity and finite existence.
