# Aristotle

Greek philosopher and polymath (384–322 BCE) whose writings covered logic, metaphysics, ethics, politics, biology, and physics. He founded the Lyceum and was tutor to Alexander the Great. His systematic treatment of logic (the Organon) and his metaphysics of substance and form shaped Western thought for two millennia. [[Zeno of Elea]] called him the inventor of dialectic.
