> "The essence of mathematics lies in its freedom."
> <cite>— Georg Cantor</cite>

---

German mathematician who founded set theory and introduced the concept of transfinite numbers, rigorously defining different sizes of infinity. His diagonal argument proved that the real numbers are uncountable, while his paradox (the set of all sets) revealed the need for axiomatic restrictions. His work was initially controversial but is now the foundation of modern mathematics.
