> # Ralph Kimball
> ###### Data Warehousing Pioneer
> | | |
> | --- | --- |
> | **Born** | 18 July 1944 |
> | **Nationality** | American |
> | **Domain** | Data warehousing, business intelligence, dimensional modeling |
> | **Known for** | Dimensional modeling; star schema; conformed dimension bus architecture; Kimball methodology |
> | **Notable works** | [[../books/the-data-warehouse-toolkit\|The Data Warehouse Toolkit]] (1996, multiple editions) |
> | **Institution** | Kimball Group (founder); Red Brick Systems (founder); ex-Xerox PARC; ex-Metaphor |
> | **Education** | PhD, Electrical Engineering (man-machine systems), Stanford University (1973) |

American author, consultant, and **pioneer of data warehousing**. Earned his **PhD in "man-machine systems"** (Electrical Engineering) from **Stanford University in 1973**. Early in his career at **Xerox PARC**, co-invented the **first commercially available workstation with a graphical user interface** — a remarkable pre-Macintosh accomplishment that reflects his lifelong focus on making complex systems understandable to human users.

Founded **Red Brick Systems** (1986), an early dedicated data warehouse database engine, and **Metaphor Computer Systems** (earlier venture). Founded the **Kimball Group** consulting firm and **Kimball University**, which taught dimensional modeling classes to over **10,000 students** worldwide over several decades.

Published *The Data Warehouse Toolkit* in **1996** — introducing the **star schema**, **fact tables**, **dimension tables**, **slowly changing dimensions (SCDs)**, and the **conformed dimension bus architecture**. These concepts form the **Kimball methodology** (bottom-up, start with individual subject-area data marts, integrate via conformed dimensions). Its counterpart is [[bill-inmon|Bill Inmon]]'s top-down 3NF EDW approach — the Inmon vs Kimball debate shaped enterprise data architecture for two decades.

## Key contributions

- **Dimensional modeling**: star schema, fact/dimension tables, SCDs, snowflake schema
- **Conformed dimension bus architecture**: integration strategy for bottom-up data warehouses
- **The Data Warehouse Toolkit** (1996, multiple editions): the most widely adopted DW methodology book
- **Kimball University**: trained 10,000+ data warehouse practitioners
- **Co-invented the first commercial GUI workstation** at Xerox PARC — before the Apple Lisa or Macintosh

## Interesting facts

- Kimball's work at **Xerox PARC** on GUI workstations predates the Apple Macintosh — he was thinking about human-computer interaction long before DW design
- The **star schema** Kimball popularized is still the dominant pattern in modern data warehouses, cloud warehouses (BigQuery, Redshift, Snowflake), and BI tools
- His emphasis that "data warehouses must be designed to be understandable and fast" was a direct rebuttal to Inmon's 3NF approach, which he argued was too complex for business users and too slow for analytics
