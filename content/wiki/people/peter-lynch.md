> # Peter Lynch
> ###### Fund Manager & Author

American investor and former fund manager who ran the **Magellan Fund** at Fidelity Investments from 1977 to 1990. Under Lynch, Magellan grew from $18M to $14B in assets and achieved a **29.2% average annual return**, making it the best-performing mutual fund in the world for that period and consistently outperforming the S&P 500.

Lynch popularized the concept of **"invest in what you know"** — the idea that retail investors have a natural edge over Wall Street analysts in identifying emerging businesses through everyday consumer experience. He classified stocks into six categories (slow growers, stalwarts, fast growers, cyclicals, asset plays, turnarounds) and advocated for thorough research combined with **GARP** (Growth at a Reasonable Price) thinking.

## Key contributions

- **Magellan Fund management**: 29.2% CAGR over 13 years; best-performing mutual fund of its era
- *One Up on Wall Street*: democratized sophisticated stock-picking analysis for individual investors
- **GARP investing**: PEG ratio as a valuation tool — price/earnings relative to earnings growth rate
- **Stock categorization framework**: 6 categories from slow growers to fast growers for portfolio strategy

## Interesting facts

- Lynch retired at 46 to spend more time with his family — one of the youngest successful retirements in fund management history
- He famously said that over 20 years the average investor can outperform most professionals if they just do their homework
- His 1989 book sold over a million copies and is still considered essential reading for retail investors
