<span class="at-kicker">Biography</span>

## Background

Slovak-Canadian computer scientist and AI educator born in **Bratislava, Slovakia**. Pursued his PhD at **Stanford University** (2011–2015) under **Fei-Fei Li**, focusing on deep learning and computer vision. Was a **founding research scientist at OpenAI** (2015–2017) — one of the original team that set the stage for GPT. Joined **Tesla** in 2017 as **Director of AI and Autopilot Vision**, leading the computer vision team behind the neural-network-based self-driving Autopilot system. Briefly rejoined OpenAI in 2023 before departing in **February 2024** to pursue independent work.

In **July 2024**, founded **Eureka Labs** — an AI-first education company combining human instruction with AI teaching assistants, embodying his vision of AI-augmented learning at scale.

Best known in the developer community for hands-on educational projects that made deep learning accessible to everyone: **CS231n** (Stanford's flagship Convolutional Neural Networks course), **nanoGPT** (a minimal but complete GPT implementation from scratch), **micrograd** (a tiny autograd engine in ~100 lines of Python), and his massively popular YouTube series *"Let's build GPT"* and *"Let's reproduce GPT-2."* His **"build it yourself"** philosophy directly inspired the structure of this wiki.

## Interesting facts

> [!grid|cols2]
>
>> [!card|section]
>> ### "Vibe Coding" Coinage
>> Coined the term **"vibe coding"** in a January 2025 tweet — describing a flow state of AI-assisted programming where you describe intent and the AI writes the code. The phrase spread instantly across the developer community.
>
>> [!card|section]
>> ### YouTube Educator at Scale
>> His *"The Spelled-Out Intro to Neural Networks and Backpropagation"* YouTube video has millions of views and is considered the clearest explanation of backprop available — bringing graduate-level concepts to anyone with a browser.

> [!quote]
> "build it yourself" — Karpathy's educational philosophy, embedded throughout his courses and projects, that the deepest understanding comes from implementing neural networks from scratch rather than using high-level abstractions.
