# Charlie Munger

American investor, businessman, and philanthropist (1924–2023) who served as vice chairman of Berkshire Hathaway alongside [[warren-buffett|Warren Buffett]]. Munger championed multidisciplinary thinking — drawing on psychology, mathematics, history, and physics — and coined the idea of investing in "wonderful companies at fair prices" rather than fair companies at wonderful prices.
