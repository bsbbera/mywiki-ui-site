> "The whole problem with the world is that fools and fanatics are always so certain of themselves, and wiser people so full of doubts."
> <cite>— Bertrand Russell</cite>

---

British polymath who reshaped logic, mathematics, and philosophy in the 20th century. His discovery of [[Russell's paradox]] in 1901 exposed a fatal contradiction in naive set theory and spurred the development of Zermelo-Fraenkel axioms and type theory. With Alfred North Whitehead, he wrote *Principia Mathematica*, an attempt to ground all mathematics in logic. He was awarded the Nobel Prize in Literature in 1950.
