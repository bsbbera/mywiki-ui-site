> "The goal of life is living in agreement with nature."
> <cite>— Zeno of Citium (not Zeno of Elea, but often conflated)</cite>

---

Pre-Socratic Greek philosopher best known for his paradoxes of motion, which argue that motion is impossible. His thought experiments — Achilles and the tortoise, the arrow in flight, the dichotomy — forced mathematicians to develop rigorous treatments of infinity, limits, and infinite series. [[Aristotle]] called him the inventor of dialectic.
