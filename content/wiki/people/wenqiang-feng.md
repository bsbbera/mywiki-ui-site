Data scientist with a **PhD in Computational Mathematics** from the **University of Tennessee, Knoxville**. Former **IMA Data Science Fellow** at the **University of Minnesota** — the Institute for Mathematics and its Applications (IMA) fellowship is a prestigious postdoctoral programme bridging academic mathematics with applied data science problems in industry.

Former member of **DST's Applied Analytics Group**, where he worked on practical data engineering and machine learning applications. Author of the widely-used free open-access tutorial **Learning Apache Spark with Python** — a comprehensive, beginner-friendly guide to [[pyspark|PySpark]] covering:

- Spark architecture and the RDD/DataFrame programming model
- Data mining and exploratory data analysis
- Text mining and NLP with Spark
- Machine learning with [[spark-mllib|MLlib]] (classification, regression, clustering, collaborative filtering)
- Real-world data pipeline patterns

The tutorial is freely available online and has been cited and used as an introductory resource by practitioners, university courses, and data engineering bootcamps worldwide.

## Key contributions

- *Learning Apache Spark with Python*: accessible, free, comprehensive PySpark learning resource
- Applied research bridging computational mathematics with practical big data engineering

## Interesting facts

- The IMA Data Science Fellowship (University of Minnesota) specifically targets PhD mathematicians transitioning into industry data science — one of the few programs that recognizes mathematical training as directly relevant to modern data problems
- Computational mathematics backgrounds are relatively rare in data engineering — they bring a rigorous understanding of numerical methods, optimization, and convergence that complements software engineering backgrounds
