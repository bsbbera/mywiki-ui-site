Iconic Google engineering duo. Co-authored the seminal Google papers that catalyzed modern data engineering:

- **GFS** (Google File System, 2003).
- **MapReduce** (2004).
- **BigTable** (2006).
- **Spanner** (2012).

These papers directly inspired Hadoop, HBase, Cassandra, and the modern cloud data stack. Both are **Google Senior Fellows**. Jeff Dean now leads Google's AI / Gemini efforts.
