# Covariance

<p class="at-lead">
Covariance measures how two random variables change together. Positive covariance indicates that variables tend to increase or decrease simultaneously; negative covariance means they tend to move in opposite directions. It is the building block of correlation, portfolio theory, and principal component analysis.
</p>

## Overview

The covariance matrix generalises this idea to multiple variables, capturing all pairwise relationships. It appears in Gaussian distributions, Mahalanobis distance, linear discriminant analysis, and the derivation of ordinary least squares. In machine learning, understanding covariance structure helps with feature decorrelation, whitening, and designing kernels for Gaussian processes.
