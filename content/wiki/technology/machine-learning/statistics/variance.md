# Variance

<p class="at-lead">
Variance measures the expected squared deviation of a random variable from its mean. It quantifies uncertainty, risk, and variability — appearing in loss functions, regularisation terms, portfolio theory, and the bias-variance decomposition that governs model generalisation.
</p>

## Overview

Population variance is computed over an entire distribution, while sample variance divides by n-1 to provide an unbiased estimate. Standard deviation, the square root of variance, expresses spread in the original units of the data. In machine learning, variance captures the sensitivity of a model to fluctuations in the training set — high variance leads to overfitting, while bias-variance trade-off analysis guides regularisation and model selection.
