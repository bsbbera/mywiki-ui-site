# Dialogue Systems

<p class="at-lead">
Dialogue Systems are AI agents designed to converse with humans in natural language. They range from simple rule-based chatbots to sophisticated large language models that maintain context, handle ambiguity, and execute complex tasks across customer service, healthcare, education, and entertainment.
</p>

## Overview

Modern dialogue systems leverage retrieval-augmented generation, reinforcement learning from human feedback (RLHF), and multi-turn context management to produce coherent, helpful, and safe responses. Key challenges include grounding in external knowledge, maintaining consistent personas, handling multi-domain conversations, and preventing harmful or hallucinated outputs.
