# Word Embeddings

<p class="at-lead">
Word embeddings are dense, low-dimensional vector representations of words that capture semantic and syntactic meaning. Words with similar meanings cluster together in vector space, making embeddings the foundational input layer for virtually all modern natural language processing systems.
</p>

## Overview

Embeddings solve the sparsity and orthogonality problems of one-hot encoding by learning continuous vectors from large text corpora. Pre-trained embeddings like Word2Vec, GloVe, and FastText can be fine-tuned for downstream tasks, while contextual embeddings from BERT and GPT dynamically adjust word meaning based on surrounding context.
