# Text Summarization

<p class="at-lead">
Text Summarization is the task of condensing a document into a shorter version while preserving its key information. Extractive methods select important sentences; abstractive methods generate novel phrasing. It powers news digests, meeting minutes, legal briefs, and search result snippets.
</p>

## Overview

Modern abstractive summarisation uses encoder-decoder Transformers and large language models fine-tuned on summary datasets like CNN/DailyMail and XSum. Challenges include factual consistency, hallucination, and handling long documents, with recent advances in long-context Transformers and retrieval-augmented generation pushing the state of the art.
