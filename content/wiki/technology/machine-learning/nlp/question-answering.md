# Question Answering

<p class="at-lead">
Question Answering is the task of automatically generating accurate answers to natural language questions, either from a provided text passage or from a large knowledge base. It is the core capability behind search engines, virtual assistants, and intelligent tutoring systems.
</p>

## Overview

Modern QA systems span extractive approaches that pinpoint answer spans in context, to generative models that synthesise answers from retrieved passages. Open-domain QA combines information retrieval with reading comprehension, while knowledge-base QA maps questions to structured queries. Transformer models fine-tuned on SQuAD, Natural Questions, and MS MARCO dominate leaderboards.
