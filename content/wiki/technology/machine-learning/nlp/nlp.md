# Natural Language Processing

<p class="at-lead">
Natural Language Processing (NLP) is the branch of artificial intelligence concerned with enabling computers to understand, interpret, and generate human language. From search engines and virtual assistants to translation and sentiment analysis, NLP powers the interfaces between people and machines.
</p>

## Overview

NLP spans a rich hierarchy of tasks: tokenisation and morphological analysis, syntactic parsing, semantic understanding, discourse processing, and language generation. Modern NLP is dominated by deep learning — particularly Transformer architectures pre-trained on massive corpora and fine-tuned for specific tasks — achieving near-human performance on benchmarks for reading comprehension, translation, and summarisation.
