# Autocorrect

<p class="at-lead">
Autocorrect is the technology that automatically detects and corrects spelling errors, typos, and grammatical mistakes in real time. It powers smartphone keyboards, word processors, email clients, and search engines, reducing friction in human-computer text interaction.
</p>

## Overview

Classical autocorrect uses edit distance, noisy channel models, and n-gram probabilities to rank candidate corrections. Modern systems augment these with neural language models that consider context, user history, and domain-specific vocabulary. Personalisation and federated learning have made autocorrect increasingly accurate without compromising privacy.
