# GPT

<p class="at-lead">
GPT (Generative Pre-trained Transformer) is a family of large language models that learns to generate coherent text by predicting the next token in a sequence. Pre-trained on vast internet-scale corpora and fine-tuned for specific tasks, GPT powers conversational AI, code generation, creative writing, and reasoning systems used by hundreds of millions of people.
</p>

## Overview

GPT models are decoder-only Transformers trained with autoregressive language modelling objectives. Scaling model size, data, and compute has produced emergent capabilities — from in-context learning and chain-of-thought reasoning to multilingual translation and code synthesis — that were not explicitly programmed but arise from statistical patterns in training data.
