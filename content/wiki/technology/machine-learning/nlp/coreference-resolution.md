# Coreference Resolution

<p class="at-lead">
Coreference Resolution is the task of identifying all expressions in a text that refer to the same real-world entity. Resolving that "she" refers to "Marie Curie" three sentences earlier is essential for document understanding, knowledge extraction, and coherent dialogue systems.
</p>

## Overview

Coreference chains link mentions — pronouns, definite descriptions, and proper names — to their antecedents. Modern systems use mention-pair scoring with pre-trained Transformers and clustering algorithms. It is a critical preprocessing step for summarisation, question answering, and information extraction pipelines.
