# Sentiment Analysis

<p class="at-lead">
Sentiment Analysis is the task of determining the emotional tone behind a piece of text — whether it is positive, negative, or neutral. It enables brands to monitor public opinion, financial analysts to gauge market mood, and customer service teams to prioritise feedback at scale.
</p>

## Overview

Sentiment analysis ranges from document-level polarity classification to aspect-based sentiment extraction that identifies opinions about specific product features. Modern approaches leverage pre-trained language models fine-tuned on labelled corpora, achieving human-level accuracy on standard benchmarks while handling sarcasm, context dependence, and domain shifts.
