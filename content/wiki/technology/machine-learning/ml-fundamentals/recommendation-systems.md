# Recommendation Systems

<p class="at-lead">
Recommendation Systems predict user preferences to suggest relevant items — movies, products, articles, or connections. They power Netflix, Amazon, Spotify, and LinkedIn, driving engagement, discovery, and revenue through personalised experiences at internet scale.
</p>

## Overview

Collaborative filtering recommends items based on user-item interaction patterns, while content-based filtering matches item attributes to user profiles. Matrix factorisation, deep learning, and sequential models have advanced the state of the art, while hybrid approaches combine multiple signals. Key challenges include cold-start problems, filter bubbles, scalability, and fairness in recommendation outcomes.
