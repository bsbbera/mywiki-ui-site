# Anomaly Detection

<p class="at-lead">
Anomaly Detection is the task of identifying patterns in data that do not conform to expected behaviour. It is critical for fraud detection, network intrusion monitoring, equipment predictive maintenance, and scientific discovery — flagging rare events that demand attention.
</p>

## Overview

Anomaly detection methods span statistical approaches (z-scores, Mahalanobis distance), clustering-based methods (DBSCAN), isolation techniques, and deep learning autoencoders that learn normal patterns and flag high-reconstruction-error inputs. The challenge lies in the inherent rarity and diversity of anomalies, which makes supervised learning difficult and demands unsupervised or semi-supervised strategies.
