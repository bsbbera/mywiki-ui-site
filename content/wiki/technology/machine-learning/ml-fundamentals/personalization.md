# Personalization

<p class="at-lead">
Personalization is the practice of tailoring products, content, and experiences to individual users based on their behaviour, preferences, and context. It spans recommendation engines, adaptive learning, personalised marketing, and dynamic pricing — creating relevance in a world of infinite choice.
</p>

## Overview

Machine learning enables personalization by learning user embeddings from implicit feedback (clicks, dwell time, purchases) and explicit feedback (ratings, surveys). Contextual bandits and reinforcement learning handle real-time adaptation, while privacy-preserving techniques like federated learning and differential privacy ensure user trust. The challenge is balancing relevance with serendipity and avoiding filter bubbles.
