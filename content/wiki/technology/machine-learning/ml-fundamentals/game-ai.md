# Game AI

<p class="at-lead">
Game AI is the application of artificial intelligence to play strategic games — from chess and Go to poker and real-time strategy. It has been a proving ground for reinforcement learning, search algorithms, and neural networks, producing systems that surpass the world's best human players.
</p>

## Overview

Game AI combines tree search (minimax, Monte Carlo Tree Search) with deep neural networks for position evaluation and policy prediction. AlphaGo, AlphaZero, and MuZero demonstrated that self-play and general-purpose learning algorithms can master complex games from scratch. Beyond entertainment, game AI research advances planning, reasoning, and multi-agent coordination with applications in robotics, logistics, and negotiation.
