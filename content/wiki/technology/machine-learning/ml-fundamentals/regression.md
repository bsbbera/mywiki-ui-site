# Regression

<p class="at-lead">
Regression is the supervised learning task of predicting a continuous numerical output from input features. It underlies demand forecasting, price estimation, risk scoring, and scientific modelling, making it one of the most foundational and widely deployed machine learning tasks.
</p>

## Overview

Regression models range from simple linear regression and polynomial fitting to sophisticated ensemble methods like gradient boosting and neural networks. Evaluation relies on metrics like mean squared error, mean absolute error, and R-squared. Key challenges include handling non-linear relationships, heteroscedasticity, outliers, and extrapolation beyond the training distribution.
