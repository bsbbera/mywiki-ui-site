# Game Theory

<p class="at-lead">
Game Theory is the mathematical study of strategic interaction among rational decision-makers. It provides the foundations for understanding competition, cooperation, and negotiation — with applications in economics, political science, biology, and multi-agent reinforcement learning.
</p>

## Overview

Key concepts include Nash equilibrium, where no player can benefit by unilaterally changing strategy; zero-sum games like poker and chess; and cooperative games where players form coalitions. In machine learning, game theory informs adversarial training, generative adversarial networks, auction design for ad placement, and the design of multi-agent reinforcement learning environments.
