# Demand Forecasting

<p class="at-lead">
Demand Forecasting is the practice of predicting future customer demand for products or services using historical data, seasonality patterns, and external variables. It drives inventory management, production planning, workforce scheduling, and financial budgeting across retail, manufacturing, and logistics.
</p>

## Overview

Forecasting methods range from classical statistical approaches (ARIMA, exponential smoothing) to machine learning (gradient boosting, neural networks) and modern deep learning architectures like DeepAR and Temporal Fusion Transformers. Accuracy metrics like MAPE, WAPE, and pinball loss guide model selection, while probabilistic forecasts enable robust inventory optimisation under uncertainty.
