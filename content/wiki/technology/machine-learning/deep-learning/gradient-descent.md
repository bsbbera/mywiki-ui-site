# Gradient Descent

<p class="at-lead">
Gradient Descent is the foundational optimisation algorithm that trains neural networks by iteratively adjusting weights in the direction that minimises loss. It is the engine behind virtually every modern deep learning system.
</p>

## Overview

At its core, gradient descent computes the gradient of a loss function with respect to the model's parameters and takes a step in the opposite direction. Variants like **Stochastic Gradient Descent (SGD)**, **Momentum**, **RMSprop**, and **Adam** have made it possible to train networks with billions of parameters reliably and efficiently.
