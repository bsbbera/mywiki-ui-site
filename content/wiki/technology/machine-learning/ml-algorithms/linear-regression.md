# Linear Regression

<p class="at-lead">
Linear Regression models the relationship between a dependent variable and one or more independent variables by fitting a linear equation to observed data. It is the simplest and most interpretable predictive model, serving as a baseline for regression tasks and a foundation for more complex algorithms.
</p>

## Overview

Ordinary Least Squares finds the line that minimises the sum of squared residuals. Extensions include multiple regression, polynomial regression, and regularised variants like Ridge and Lasso. Despite its simplicity, linear regression remains powerful when relationships are approximately linear and features are carefully engineered — and its coefficients offer direct interpretability that complex models often lack.
