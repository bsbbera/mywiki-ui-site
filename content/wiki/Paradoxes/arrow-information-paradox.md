# Arrow Information Paradox

<p class="at-lead">
How can a seller prove the value of information without destroying exclusivity? Once the buyer knows the information, they have no need to pay for it. Markets for information are inherently incomplete, motivating intellectual-property mechanisms that create artificial scarcity.
</p>

## Overview

Kenneth Arrow founded modern information economics with this paradox. Because information is non-rivalrous and difficult to exclude, standard market mechanisms fail. The paradox underpins arguments for intellectual-property rights, open-source business models, and the vertical integration of R&D within firms.
