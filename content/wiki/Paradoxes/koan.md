# Koan

<p class="at-lead">
A koan is a paradoxical anecdote, question, or statement used in Zen Buddhist practice to short-circuit discursive thinking and provoke direct, non-conceptual insight. Famous examples include "What is the sound of one hand clapping?" and "Does a dog have Buddha-nature?" The koan is not a puzzle to solve but a technique to exhaust the intellect.
</p>

## Overview

Zen masters assign koans to students during meditation and daily life. The apparent demand for a rational answer is a trap; genuine response emerges only when conceptual reasoning collapses. The paradox invites the mind to exhaust itself, so that something beyond intellect — satori or kensho — might emerge.
