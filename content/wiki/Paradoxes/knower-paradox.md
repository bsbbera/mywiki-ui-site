# Knower Paradox

<p class="at-lead">
"This sentence is not known." If it is known, it is true, so it is not known — contradiction. Epistemic self-reference creates the same structure as the liar paradox, but for knowledge instead of truth. Formal epistemic logic must restrict self-referential knowledge claims.
</p>

## Overview

The knower paradox is the epistemic twin of the liar paradox. Just as Tarski blocked the liar with a hierarchy of truth predicates, epistemic logicians block the knower with a hierarchy of knowledge operators. The paradox shows that self-referential knowledge claims cannot be consistently formalised within a single language level.
