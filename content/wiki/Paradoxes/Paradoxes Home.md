# Paradoxes

<p class="at-lead">A paradox is a statement, proposition, or situation that seems to yield a self-contradictory or logically unacceptable conclusion. Across the canon, paradoxes fall into three fundamental kinds: the <em>veridical</em> paradox — a result that appears absurd yet is demonstrably true; the <em>falsidical</em> paradox — a conclusion that rests on concealed fallacious reasoning; and the <em>antinomy</em> — a genuine contradiction produced by accepted methods of deduction. This vault now holds roughly three hundred paradoxes across seventeen thematic notes, each one a probe into the boundaries of coherent thought.</p>
