# Fermi Paradox

<p class="at-lead">
With billions of stars in the Milky Way, many with potentially habitable planets, why have we observed no signs of alien civilisations? The silence of the cosmos screams a question that touches astronomy, biology, and the future of intelligence.
</p>

## Overview

The Fermi paradox arises from the apparent contradiction between the high probability of extraterrestrial civilisations and the lack of evidence for them. Proposed resolutions range from the Great Filter (civilisations destroy themselves before achieving interstellar travel) to the Zoo Hypothesis (aliens deliberately avoid contact) to simple rarity of technological life.
