# Acosmism

<p class="at-lead">
Acosmism is the view that the finite, created world is ultimately illusory or non-existent when measured against the absolute reality of the Divine. In Lurianic Kabbalah, Upper Unity sees Creation as acosmic — nullified before the infinite light of Ein Sof — while Lower Unity affirms self-aware existence from the creaturely perspective.
</p>

## Overview

The tension between acosmism and theism generates the paradox of Tzimtzum: if God is infinite, how can anything else exist? Acosmism resolves this by denying the ultimate reality of the "anything else." Yet this denial itself must coexist with the lived experience of creation, producing two simultaneous modes of truth that logical contradiction cannot dissolve.
