# Transcendence

<p class="at-lead">
Divine transcendence is the property of being entirely beyond and independent of the material universe. In classical theism, God is not part of creation and cannot be limited by it. Yet transcendence must coexist with immanence — God's presence within creation — producing a living paradox central to mystical and philosophical theology.
</p>

## Overview

The tension between transcendence and immanence structures much of religious metaphysics. In Lurianic Kabbalah, Upper Unity emphasises transcendence: from the divine perspective, nothing truly exists apart from God (acosmism). Lower Unity emphasises immanence: from the creaturely perspective, creation is real and precious. God encompasses both simultaneously.
