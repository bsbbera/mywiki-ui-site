# Immanence

<p class="at-lead">
Divine immanence is the property of being present within and throughout the material universe. While transcendence places God beyond creation, immanence places God within it — not as a distant watchmaker but as the sustaining ground of every existing thing. The coexistence of immanence and transcendence is a central paradox of mystical theology.
</p>

## Overview

In Hasidic panentheism, Lower Unity affirms immanence: from the creaturely perspective, self-aware existence is real and encompassed by the Divine. God is not merely external to the world but its inner life. This view echoes Spinoza's Deus sive Natura and process theology, yet maintains the distinction between Creator and creature.
