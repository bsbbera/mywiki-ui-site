# Barber Paradox

<p class="at-lead">
A male barber shaves all and only those men who do not shave themselves. Does he shave himself? If he does, he doesn't; if he doesn't, he does. Russell's popularization of his set-theoretic paradox exposes the impossibility of unrestricted self-reference.
</p>

## Overview

The Barber paradox is a vivid illustration of Russell's paradox cast in everyday terms. It demonstrates that naive set construction — defining a set by any property whatsoever — leads to contradiction. The resolution, developed in axiomatic set theory, is to forbid unrestricted comprehension: a set cannot be guaranteed to exist merely because we can describe its members.
