# Sorites Paradox

<p class="at-lead">
The Sorites paradox asks at what precise grain a heap becomes not-a-heap. Classical logic demands that every statement be true or false, yet vague predicates like "heap" seem to lack sharp boundaries. The paradox has driven theories of supervaluationism, degree theories, and fuzzy logic.
</p>

## Overview

Also called the paradox of the heap, the Sorites challenges bivalence — the principle that every proposition is either true or false. Incremental change appears to preserve category membership at each step, yet the accumulated steps eventually produce a category shift. Resolutions either accept fuzzy truth-values or deny that vague predicates pick out precise properties.
