# Flash Lag Illusion

<p class="at-lead">
A flashing stimulus is presented in physical alignment with a moving object, yet the flash appears to lag behind the moving object. This perceptual delay reveals that the brain is not a passive camera but a predictive engine, extrapolating future positions to compensate for neural transmission delays.
</p>

## Overview

The flash lag illusion demonstrates predictive coding in the visual system. Because moving objects follow predictable trajectories, the brain "pushes forward" their perceived position. A sudden flash has no trajectory, so it is not anticipated and appears to lag behind. This exposes the forward-model at work in visual awareness.
