# Kabbalah

<p class="at-lead">
Kabbalah is the esoteric tradition of Jewish mysticism, centred on the nature of the Divine, the structure of creation, and the soul's journey. Its most influential system, Lurianic Kabbalah, introduces *tzimtzum* — a primordial divine contraction — and the paradoxical coexistence of infinite transcendence and finite immanence.
</p>

## Overview

Kabbalistic cosmology describes ten sefirot (divine emanations) through which the infinite Ein Sof interacts with creation. The doctrine of tzimtzum raises a profound paradox: if God is truly infinite, there is no "outside" in which creation could stand. Luria's solution — a voluntary withdrawal — maintains both divine infinity and the reality of finite existence.
