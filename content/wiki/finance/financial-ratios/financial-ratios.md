# Financial Ratios

<p class="at-lead">
Financial ratios transform raw accounting numbers into meaningful signals — they reveal profitability, solvency, efficiency, and valuation in a form that can be compared across time and between companies. Four families, dozens of ratios, one purpose: understanding what a business is truly doing with its capital.
</p>

<span class="at-stat">4 families</span> of ratios &nbsp;·&nbsp; <span class="at-stat">Profitability · Leverage · Operating · Valuation</span> &nbsp;·&nbsp; <span class="at-mark">the analyst's toolkit for business evaluation</span>

<span class="at-kicker">Four Ratio Families</span>

## The Four Families

> [!grid|cols2]
>
>> [!card|section]
>> ###### PROFITABILITY RATIOS
>> ### *Profitability* Ratios
>> Measure the company's ability to generate profits relative to revenue, assets, or equity. Signal competitiveness and management quality. Higher is almost always better.
>> **Key ratios:** EBITDA Margin, Net Margin, ROE, ROA, ROCE
>> → [[profitability-ratios|Profitability Ratios]]
>
>> [!card|section]
>> ###### LEVERAGE RATIOS
>> ### *Leverage* Ratios
>> Also called solvency or gearing ratios. Measure how much debt the company uses to finance growth and its ability to service that debt long-term.
>> **Key ratios:** Debt/Equity, Debt/Assets, Interest Coverage, Financial Leverage
>> → [[leverage-ratios|Leverage Ratios]]
>
>> [!card|section]
>> ###### OPERATING RATIOS
>> ### *Operating* Ratios
>> Also called Activity or Management ratios. Measure how efficiently the company converts assets into revenue. Higher turnover = better asset utilisation.
>> **Key ratios:** Inventory Turnover, DSO, Total Asset Turnover, Working Capital Turnover
>> → [[operating-ratios|Operating Ratios]]
>
>> [!card|section]
>> ###### VALUATION RATIOS
>> ### *Valuation* Ratios
>> Compare stock price to profitability or company value metrics. Help determine if a stock is cheap or expensive relative to fundamentals.
>> **Key ratios:** P/E, PEG, P/B, P/S, EV/EBITDA, Dividend Yield
>> → [[../investing/valuation-ratios|Valuation Ratios]]

## Complete Ratio Reference Table

| Ratio | Formula | Family | What It Signals |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **EBITDA Margin** | EBITDA / Operating Revenue | Profitability | Operating efficiency |
| **Net Margin (PAT Margin)** | PAT / Total Revenue | Profitability | Overall profitability |
| **ROE** | Net Profit / Shareholders' Equity × 100 | Profitability | Return to shareholders |
| **ROA** | (Net Income + Interest×(1−Tax)) / Avg Total Assets | Profitability | Asset productivity |
| **ROCE** | EBIT / Capital Employed | Profitability | Capital efficiency |
| **Gross Profit Margin** | Gross Profit / Revenue | Profitability | Competitive moat signal |
| **Debt/Equity** | Total Debt / Total Equity | Leverage | Financial risk |
| **Debt/Assets** | Total Debt / Total Assets | Leverage | Asset financing via debt |
| **Interest Coverage** | EBIT / Interest Payment | Leverage | Debt service capacity |
| **Financial Leverage** | Avg Total Assets / Avg Total Equity | Leverage | Equity vs. asset funding |
| **Current Ratio** | Current Assets / Current Liabilities | Leverage | Short-term liquidity |
| **Inventory Turnover** | COGS / Avg Inventory | Operating | Inventory efficiency |
| **Inventory Days** | 365 / Inventory Turnover | Operating | Days to sell inventory |
| **Accounts Receivable Turnover** | Revenue / Avg Receivables | Operating | Cash collection speed |
| **DSO** | 365 / AR Turnover | Operating | Days to collect cash |
| **Working Capital Turnover** | Revenue / Avg Working Capital | Operating | Sales vs. working capital |
| **Total Asset Turnover** | Operating Revenue / Avg Total Assets | Operating | Overall asset efficiency |
| **P/E Ratio** | Share Price / EPS | Valuation | Price per unit of earnings |
| **PEG Ratio** | P/E / EPS Growth Rate | Valuation | P/E adjusted for growth |
| **P/B Ratio** | Share Price / Book Value per Share | Valuation | Price vs. net asset value |
| **P/S Ratio** | Share Price / Sales per Share | Valuation | Price vs. revenue |
| **EV/EBITDA** | Enterprise Value / EBITDA | Valuation | Debt-inclusive valuation |
| **Dividend Yield** | Annual Dividend / Share Price | Valuation | Income return from stock |
| **EPS** | (Net Profit − Preferred Div.) / Shares | Valuation | Per-share profitability |

<span class="at-kicker">When to Use What</span>

## When to Use Which Family

> [!grid|cols2]
>
>> [!card|section]
>> ###### PROFITABILITY — USE WHEN
>> ### Assessing *Business Quality*
>> - Comparing two companies in the same sector
>> - Evaluating management's capital allocation skills
>> - Looking for consistent compounders for long-term holding
>> - Screening for competitive moats
>
>> [!card|section]
>> ###### LEVERAGE — USE WHEN
>> ### Assessing *Financial Risk*
>> - Evaluating companies in capital-intensive industries
>> - Stress-testing a company's ability to survive downturns
>> - Comparing debt levels to earnings power
>> - Deciding if a cyclical company is too risky at peak cycle

> [!grid|cols2]
>
>> [!card|section]
>> ###### OPERATING — USE WHEN
>> ### Assessing *Management Efficiency*
>> - Comparing operational efficiency within an industry
>> - Detecting inventory build-up warning signs
>> - Identifying companies with superior receivables management
>> - Evaluating asset utilisation over time
>
>> [!card|section]
>> ###### VALUATION — USE WHEN
>> ### Assessing *Price vs. Value*
>> - Deciding if a stock is cheap or expensive
>> - Comparing valuation across an industry peer group
>> - Combining with quality metrics (P/E + ROE = quality value)
>> - Screening for undervalued stocks with good fundamentals

## Ratio-Based Stock Screening Criteria

When screening for quality stocks, a practical starting framework:

| Criteria | Threshold | Ratio Used |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Size** | Market Cap > ₹6,000 Cr | — |
| **Revenue Growth** | 3-year CAGR > 7% | Revenue trend |
| **Profit Growth** | 3-year CAGR > 7% | PAT trend |
| **Capital Use** | ROCE > 15% | [[profitability-ratios\|ROCE]] |
| **Debt Safety** | Debt/Equity < 0.5 | [[leverage-ratios\|D/E Ratio]] |
| **Valuation** | Current P/E < 1.5× 5-year avg P/E | [[../investing/valuation-ratios\|P/E]] |
| **Growth Valuation** | PEG < 2 (lower is better) | [[../investing/valuation-ratios\|PEG]] |

> [!tip] Context is Everything
> No ratio should be evaluated in isolation. Always:
> 1. **Compare to historical values** for the same company (trend matters)
> 2. **Compare to industry peers** (sector norms vary wildly)
> 3. **Use multiple ratios together** — one ratio can be gamed; a full picture cannot

> [!warning] Ratio Limitations
> - ROE can be inflated by high debt (DuPont analysis helps decompose it)
> - P/E ratios are meaningless for loss-making companies
> - EBITDA ignores real cash costs like CapEx — use FCF alongside it
> - Current ratio < 1 isn't always bad (strong companies can tap commercial paper)
