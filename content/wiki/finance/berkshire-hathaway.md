# Berkshire Hathaway

<p class="at-lead">
Berkshire Hathaway is an American multinational conglomerate holding company headquartered in Omaha, Nebraska. Led by Warren Buffett since 1965, it is one of the largest and most closely watched companies in the world, owning stakes in insurance, railroads, utilities, and consumer brands.
</p>

## Overview

Berkshire's business model centres on using **float** (premiums collected by insurance subsidiaries like GEICO and General Re) to invest in wholly owned businesses and publicly traded equities. Its portfolio spans BNSF Railway, Berkshire Hathaway Energy, Dairy Queen, See's Candies, and major stakes in Apple, Coca-Cola, Bank of America, and American Express.

The company's annual shareholder meetings in Omaha draw tens of thousands of investors. Buffett's annual letters are widely read for their wit, clarity, and investment wisdom. Charlie Munger served as vice chairman until his death in 2023, deeply influencing Berkshire's culture of rationality and patience.
