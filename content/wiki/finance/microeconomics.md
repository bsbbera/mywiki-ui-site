# Microeconomics

<p class="at-lead">
Microeconomics is the branch of economics that studies the behaviour of individuals, households, and firms in making decisions about the allocation of limited resources. It examines how prices are determined in markets and how incentives shape behaviour.
</p>

## Overview

Core topics include **supply and demand**, **elasticity**, **consumer theory** (utility maximisation, indifference curves), **producer theory** (cost curves, profit maximisation), **market structures** (perfect competition, monopoly, oligopoly, monopolistic competition), and **game theory** (strategic interaction, Nash equilibrium).

Microeconomics provides the analytical foundation for business strategy, public policy, and finance. Concepts like marginal analysis, opportunity cost, and market efficiency are essential for understanding pricing, investment, and regulation.
