> # Learning Apache Spark with Python
> ###### Online Tutorial
> | | |
> | --- | --- |
> | **Author** | [[wenqiang-feng\|Wenqiang Feng]] |
> | **Format** | Free online document (Sphinx) |

A **free, self-teaching tutorial** on [[apache-spark|Apache Spark]] with [[pyspark|PySpark]], written by Wenqiang Feng during his IMA Data Science Fellowship. It walks from Spark setup and [[rdd|RDD]]/DataFrame fundamentals through a large applied-ML section ([[spark-mllib|MLlib]]): regression, classification, clustering, text mining, recommendations (ALS), Monte Carlo, and neural networks. Demos run on [[databricks|Databricks]] Community Cloud.

Official document: <https://runawayhorse001.github.io/LearningApacheSpark/>. *(No conventional book cover — distributed as a living PDF / HTML doc.)*
