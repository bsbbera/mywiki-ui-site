Author: [[../people/martin-kleppmann|Martin Kleppmann]] · Publisher: O'Reilly · 2017

The **definitive modern reference** for building reliable, scalable, maintainable distributed data systems. Covers replication, partitioning, transactions, consistency, consensus, batch + stream processing.

Often shortened to **DDIA**. Considered required reading for serious data engineers and distributed-systems engineers.
