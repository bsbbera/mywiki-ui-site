# Biology

<p class="at-lead">
Biology is the natural science that studies life and living organisms — their structure, function, growth, origin, evolution, and distribution. From molecular genetics to ecosystem ecology, biology spans scales from individual atoms to the entire biosphere.
</p>

## Overview

Major branches include **molecular biology** (DNA, RNA, proteins), **cell biology** (structure and function of cells), **genetics** (inheritance and variation), **physiology** (organism function), **evolutionary biology** (species change over time), **ecology** (organism-environment interactions), and **taxonomy** (classification of life).

Biology underpins medicine, agriculture, biotechnology, and conservation. Modern biology is increasingly data-driven, with genomics, proteomics, and computational biology generating enormous datasets analysed with machine learning and statistical methods.
