# Shepard Tone

<p class="at-lead">
A Shepard tone is a sound consisting of a superposition of sine waves separated by octaves, with the bass fading in and the treble fading out. The result is an auditory illusion of a tone that ascends forever in pitch without ever getting higher — a sonic staircase.
</p>

## Overview

The Shepard tone exploits the circular pitch class of the chromatic scale and the ear's limited spectral resolution. It appears in sound design for tension and suspense (notably in Dunkirk) and is central to the tritone paradox, where the same interval is heard as ascending by some listeners and descending by others.
