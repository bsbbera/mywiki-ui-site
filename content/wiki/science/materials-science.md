# Materials Science

<p class="at-lead">
Materials Science is an interdisciplinary field that studies the properties of matter and their applications to science, engineering, and technology. It bridges physics, chemistry, and engineering to design materials with novel or improved properties.
</p>

## Overview

Materials are classified by structure and bonding: metals, ceramics, polymers, composites, semiconductors, and nanomaterials. Understanding structure-property relationships enables engineers to tailor strength, conductivity, optical properties, and biocompatibility for specific applications.

Major research frontiers include **two-dimensional materials** (graphene, MXenes), **high-temperature superconductors**, **battery electrode materials** (solid-state lithium, sodium-ion), **biomaterials** (tissue scaffolds, drug-delivery systems), and **computational materials science** (machine learning for materials discovery).
