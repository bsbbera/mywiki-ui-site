# Anfinsen's Dogma

<p class="at-lead">
Anfinsen's dogma states that the three-dimensional native structure of a protein is determined entirely by its amino-acid sequence and the physiological conditions of the solvent. Christian Anfinsen won the 1972 Nobel Prize in Chemistry for work on ribonuclease that established this principle, now foundational for structural biology and protein-folding prediction.
</p>

## Overview

Also called the thermodynamic hypothesis, the dogma asserts that proteins fold spontaneously into their lowest free-energy conformations. While the Levinthal paradox once seemed to challenge this by showing that random search would take longer than the age of the universe, modern understanding reveals that proteins navigate funneled energy landscapes rather than searching exhaustively.
