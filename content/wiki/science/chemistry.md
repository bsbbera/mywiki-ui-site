# Chemistry

<p class="at-lead">
Chemistry is the physical science that studies the composition, structure, properties, and reactions of matter. It bridges physics and biology, explaining everything from atomic bonding to metabolic pathways and materials design.
</p>

## Overview

Core branches include **organic chemistry** (carbon compounds), **inorganic chemistry** (metals and minerals), **physical chemistry** (thermodynamics, kinetics, quantum chemistry), **analytical chemistry** (measurement and detection), and **biochemistry** (chemistry of living systems). Chemistry drives pharmaceuticals, energy storage, nanotechnology, and environmental science.

Modern chemistry relies heavily on computational modelling (molecular dynamics, density functional theory) and high-throughput experimentation. Green chemistry seeks sustainable processes that minimise waste and toxicity.
