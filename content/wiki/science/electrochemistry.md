# Electrochemistry

<p class="at-lead">
Electrochemistry studies the relationship between electrical energy and chemical change — how electrons drive reactions at electrodes and how reactions generate electric current. It underpins batteries, fuel cells, corrosion science, and electroplating, and overturns naive assumptions about concentration and reactivity.
</p>

## Overview

At the heart of electrochemistry are reduction–oxidation (redox) reactions, where electron transfer occurs across an interface. The Faraday paradox of electrochemistry shows that concentrated nitric acid can passivate steel while dilute acid attacks it — a demonstration that electrode kinetics and surface films matter as much as bulk concentration.
