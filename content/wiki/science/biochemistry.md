# Biochemistry

<p class="at-lead">
Biochemistry explores the chemical processes within and related to living organisms. It combines biology and chemistry to investigate the molecular mechanisms of life — from DNA replication and protein synthesis to metabolism and signal transduction.
</p>

## Overview

Key domains include **protein biochemistry** (structure, folding, enzymes), **nucleic acid biochemistry** (DNA/RNA structure, replication, transcription, translation), **metabolism** (energy pathways like glycolysis, Krebs cycle, oxidative phosphorylation), and **molecular signalling** (hormones, receptors, second messengers).

Biochemistry is foundational to medicine (drug design, diagnostics), biotechnology (recombinant DNA, CRISPR), and agriculture (crop improvement, pest resistance). Techniques like mass spectrometry, X-ray crystallography, and cryo-EM have revolutionised our ability to visualise and manipulate biomolecules.
