# General Relativity

<p class="at-lead">
Einstein's general theory of relativity (1915) describes gravity not as a force but as the curvature of spacetime caused by mass and energy. The Einstein field equations relate the geometry of spacetime to the distribution of matter, predicting black holes, gravitational lensing, gravitational waves, and the expanding universe.
</p>

## Overview

General relativity resolves Newtonian paradoxes such as Olbers' paradox and Bentley's paradox by replacing infinite Euclidean space with a finite-age, expanding spacetime metric. The theory has passed every experimental test — from Mercury's perihelion precession to LIGO's detection of gravitational waves — and underpins modern cosmology.
