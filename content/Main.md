## Featured Article

> [!infobox|wikipedia]

> # Gandalf
> 
> ![[gandalf.png|cover hsmall]]
> 
> ###### Quick Facts
> 
> |Field|Value|
> |---|---|
> |**Other names**|Mithrandir, Olórin, Tharkûn|
> |**Race**|Maia (Istar)|
> |**Affiliation**|Fellowship of the Ring|
> |**Weapon**|Glamdring, Narya|

**Gandalf** was one of the five [[Istari]] sent to Middle-earth by the [[Valar]] in the Third Age. In [[Valinor]] he was known as Olórin. He was instrumental in the demise of [[Sauron]] in T.A. 3019, chiefly by encouraging others and dispensing wisdom at pivotal times. Originally robed in grey and second to [[Saruman]] in the Order of Wizards, after his fall in [[Moria]] Gandalf returned as head of the Order, robed in white.

## Featured Image

> [!captions] ![[heraldic-devices.png|hsmall]] **Heraldic devices, emblems and flags in the legendarium.** A study in the visual symbolism of the houses of Men, Elves, and Dwarves, including the white tree of Gondor, the swan-prow of Dol Amroth, the silver harp of Finrod Felagund, and the hammer of Telchar. Heraldry in Tolkien's work reflects both his philological interests and his deep affection for medieval craft.

## Featured Artist

> [!aside|right] **Eric Velhagen** is a talented American fantasy artist whose work captures the mythic grandeur of Tolkien's world. His paintings range from intimate character studies to sweeping battle panoramas.

Tolkien's legendarium has inspired generations of artists. From the early illustrations by [[Pauline Baynes]], approved by Tolkien himself, through the cinematic vision of [[John Howe]] and [[Alan Lee]] for the Peter Jackson films, the visual tradition is rich and varied.
