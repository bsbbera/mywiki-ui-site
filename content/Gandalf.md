**Gandalf** was one of the five [[Istari]] sent to Middle-earth by the [[Valar]] in the Third Age. In [[Valinor]] he was known as **Olórin**. Gandalf was instrumental in bringing about the demise of [[Sauron]] in T.A. 3019, chiefly by encouraging others and dispensing his wisdom at pivotal times. He was originally robed in grey, and second to [[Saruman]] in the Order of Wizards. After his fall in [[Moria]], Gandalf returned to Middle-earth as head of the Order, robed in white.

## History

### Origins in Valinor

In Valinor, Gandalf was known as **Olórin**, one of the [[Maiar]] of the people of [[Manwë]] and [[Varda]]. He dwelt in the gardens of [[Irmo]] and was a pupil of [[Nienna]], the patron of mercy, from whom he learned pity and patience.[^1]

> [!quote | n-t]
> *Wisest of the Maiar was Olórin. He too dwelt in Lórien, but his ways took him often to the house of Nienna, and of her he learned pity and patience.*
> — *The Silmarillion*, "Valaquenta"

### Arrival in Middle-earth

> [!timeline|t-l] **c. T.A. 1000** *Arrival of the Istari*
> The five Istari arrive at the Grey Havens. Olórin, last to come, is welcomed by [[Círdan]].

> [!timeline|t-r t-3] **T.A. 1000** *The Gift of Narya*
> Círdan, perceiving great strength beneath Gandalf's humble appearance, gives him [[Narya]], the Ring of Fire.

[[Círdan]] divined in Olórin a sense of strength and power despite his appearance as a bent and aged old man. Círdan gave Narya to Olórin with a prediction of his future struggles with evil, and a promise that it would support and aid him in his labours.

> [!quote|no-t]
> *"Take this ring, Master, for your labours will be heavy; but it will support you in the weariness that you have taken upon yourself. For this is the Ring of Fire, and with it you may rekindle hearts in a world that grows chill."*
> — Círdan to Gandalf

### The Long Watch

Gandalf began his sojourn in Middle-earth, going about as a wanderer and counsellor. Unlike Saruman, Gandalf did not take up a single permanent residence. He never went to the east and apparently restricted his activities to the Westlands of Middle-earth, where the remnants of the [[Dúnedain]] and the [[Eldar]] remained to oppose Sauron.

> [!info|c-yellow] He was known by many names
> Elves named him **Mithrandir** ("Grey Pilgrim"); the men of Arnor named him **Gandalf**, which became his most common name. He was also known as **Incánus** in the south, and **Tharkûn** ("Staff-man") to the Dwarves.

Around T.A. 2063, Gandalf went to [[Dol Guldur]] to discover the identity of its master. The Necromancer withdrew before him, hiding in the East. This began the **Watchful Peace** that lasted until T.A. 2460.

### The Quest of Erebor

> [!timeline|t-l] **T.A. 2941** *Meeting with Thorin*
> Gandalf encounters [[Thorin Oakenshield]] in [[Bree]] and conceives the plan to reclaim [[Erebor]].

> [!timeline|t-r t-2] **T.A. 2941** *Bag End*
> Gandalf marks Bilbo's door and arranges the "Unexpected Party."

> [!timeline|t-l t-4] **T.A. 2941** *Battle of Five Armies*
> Gandalf participates in the climactic battle outside Erebor.

### The Hunt for Gollum

Gandalf grew suspicious of [[Bilbo]]'s ring. With [[Aragorn]]'s help, he searched for [[Gollum]] for many years. Gollum was eventually captured and questioned by Gandalf in T.A. 3017, confirming the Ring's identity.

### The War of the Ring

> [!recite]
> The story of the War of the Ring, in which Gandalf played the central role of organizer, counsellor, and warrior, is recounted in *The Lord of the Rings*. From his imprisonment atop [[Orthanc]] by Saruman, to his fall against the [[Balrog]] in Moria, to his return as the White and his confrontation with the [[Witch-king]] before the gates of [[Minas Tirith]], Gandalf's role is woven throughout.

#### Key Events of the War

> [!timeline|t-l] **April 12, T.A. 3018** *Capture by Saruman*
> Imprisoned atop Orthanc after refusing to join Saruman.

> [!timeline|t-r t-3] **September 18, T.A. 3018** *Escape on Gwaihir*
> Rescued from Orthanc by the eagle [[Gwaihir]].

> [!timeline|t-l t-5] **October 24, T.A. 3018** *Council of Elrond*
> Reveals the truth of Saruman's treachery in [[Rivendell]].

> [!timeline|t-r t-7] **January 15, T.A. 3019** *Fall in Moria*
> Gandalf falls with the Balrog into the abyss of Khazad-dûm.

> [!timeline|t-l t-9] **February 14, T.A. 3019** *Return as the White*
> Sent back to complete his mission. Reunites with the Three Hunters in [[Fangorn]].

> [!timeline|t-r t-11] **March 15, T.A. 3019** *The Pelennor Fields*
> Confronts the Witch-king at the gates of Minas Tirith.

> [!timeline|t-l t-13] **March 25, T.A. 3019** *Destruction of the Ring*
> The Ring is unmade at Mount Doom. Gandalf rescues Frodo and Sam from the slopes.

### Departure

> [!timeline|t-r t-15] **September 29, T.A. 3021** *The Last Ship*
> Gandalf departs Middle-earth from the Grey Havens, bearing Narya, alongside [[Frodo]], [[Bilbo]], [[Elrond]], and [[Galadriel]].

## Powers and Abilities

> [!metadata]
> **Wisdom:** Considered the wisest of the Maiar
> **Fire:** Mastery of fire, both literal and metaphorical (kindling hearts)
> **Light:** Could produce blinding flashes, particularly with his staff
> **Combat:** Skilled swordsman with [[Glamdring]]
> **Protection:** Held [[Narya]], one of the [[Three Rings of the Elves]]
> **Influence:** Could rally allies through speech and presence
> **Knowledge:** Vast learning in lore, history, languages, and ancient script

> [!info|c-orange] Constraints of the Istari
> The Istari were forbidden by the Valar to use their powers to dominate other wills, or to match Sauron's force with force. Gandalf's role was that of an **encourager and counsellor**, not a conqueror — a constraint Saruman ultimately broke.

> [!quote]
> *I am a servant of the Secret Fire, wielder of the flame of Anor. You cannot pass. The dark fire will not avail you, flame of Udûn. Go back to the Shadow! You cannot pass.*
> — Gandalf to the Balrog, *The Fellowship of the Ring*

## Other Names

> [!metadata|i-at]
> **Olórin** — His original Quenya name in Valinor, meaning "dreams" or related to *olos* (dream, vision)
> **Mithrandir** — Sindarin, "Grey Pilgrim" or "Grey Wanderer"
> **Gandalf** — Westron rendering, ultimately from Old Norse meaning "Wand-elf" or "Staff-elf"
> **Tharkûn** — Khuzdul, "Staff-man"
> **Incánus** — Used "in the South," meaning uncertain; possibly Quenya for "mind-master"
> **Greyhame** — Rohirric, "Grey-mantle"
> **Stormcrow** — A name given mockingly by [[Théoden]], used for one who brings ill tidings
> **The White Rider** — After his return, mounted on [[Shadowfax]]
> **Láthspell** — "Ill-news," another mocking name from [[Gríma Wormtongue]]

## Etymology

The name **Gandalf** is taken from the Old Norse *Dvergatal* (Catalogue of Dwarves) in the *Völuspá* of the *Poetic Edda*. The name combines *gandr* (staff, wand, or magic) with *álfr* (elf), giving a meaning of "Staff-elf" or "Wand-elf."

> [!info|alt-line] Tolkien's Source
> Tolkien noted that he chose the name because, in the Old Norse sources, Gandalf is the only name in the dwarf-list with the *-álfr* suffix, suggesting a being not strictly a dwarf — fitting for a wizard.

| Language | Name | Meaning |
| ---- | ---- | ---- |
| Quenya | Olórin | "Dreamer" |
| Sindarin | Mithrandir | "Grey Pilgrim" |
| Westron | Gandalf | "Staff-elf" |
| Khuzdul | Tharkûn | "Staff-man" |
| Rohirric | Greyhame | "Grey-mantle" |
